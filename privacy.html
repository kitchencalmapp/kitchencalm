Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 May 2026

Kitchen Calm is a free cooking app built for halal households. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What data we collect

Kitchen Calm stores data only on your device using your browser's local storage. This includes:

  • Your pantry/cupboard selections
  • Your meal history and favourites
  • Your filter and preference settings
  • Your cook streak count

This data never leaves your device. We have no access to it and it is not transmitted to any server.

Feedback form

If you choose to submit feedback through the app, your message is sent to us via Formspree (formspree.io). Formspree processes and stores the submission on our behalf. We only receive the content of your message and any email address you choose to provide. We use this solely to respond to your feedback.

Formspree's privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how users interact with the app. Clarity may collect anonymised usage data including pages visited, taps, and scrolling behaviour. It does not collect personally identifiable information. No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Microsoft Clarity's privacy policy is available at privacy.microsoft.com.

Cookies

Kitchen Calm does not use cookies. We use browser local storage only to save your preferences on your device.

Children

Kitchen Calm is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

Third-party links

The app does not contain links to third-party websites or services beyond what is described above.

Your rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for kitchencalm.app. You do not need to contact us to do this.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at feedback@kitchencalm.app.
